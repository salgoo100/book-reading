3. In a green field, a cow and a horse chase the Gingerbread Man, who runs ahead laughing; dust puffs behind them.
4. At the edge of a wide river, the Gingerbread Man stops; the fox sits by the water, smiling slyly and offering his back.
5. In the middle of the river, the fox swims with only his head above water; the Gingerbread Man stands on the fox's nose, looking nervous.
6. Comic ending, not scary: the fox tosses the Gingerbread Man into the air with his nose, mouth wide open to catch him; only a few crumbs flying; playful slapstick feel.
