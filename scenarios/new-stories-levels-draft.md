# 새 이야기 4편: Level 2·3 원고 초안

원칙: **줄거리는 원작 그대로, 표현만 부드럽게.** 한국 전래동화는 한국어 그림책 줄거리와 같게 맞춰요.
- 그림 표시: `s1`~`s6`는 지금 있는 Level 1 그림(`images/<story>/sceneN.webp`)이고, `🆕A`~`🆕D`는 새로 그릴 그림이에요.
- Level 2: 과거형, 8장면. Level 3: 이어진 문장과 감정, 10장면.
- 확정되면 한국어 힌트, 🎭 Why, 💬 Talk, ❓ Quiz를 붙여서 앱에 넣고, Codex 지시문과 음성을 만들어요.

## 🐻 Goldilocks and the Three Bears — 새 그림 4장
원작의 핵심인 곰들의 반복 대사 *"Someone's been eating my porridge!"* 를 Level 2부터 넣었어요.

| 새 그림 | 장면 |
|---|---|
| 🆕A `bears-return` | 집에 돌아온 곰 가족이 식탁의 빈 죽 그릇을 보고 놀라는 장면 (아기 곰은 빈 그릇을 들고 울상) |
| 🆕B `bears-chairs` | 곰 가족이 의자를 살펴보고, 아기 곰이 부서진 작은 의자를 가리키는 장면 |
| 🆕C `bears-walk` | 곰 가족이 숲속을 산책하고, 아기 곰이 산딸기를 따는 장면 |
| 🆕D `runs-away` | 골디락스가 숲길을 뒤도 안 돌아보고 달려가고, 멀리 오두막 창문에서 곰들이 내다보는 장면 |

### 🌿 Level 2 (8)
| # | 그림 | 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s1 | The three bears made porridge. It was too hot, so they went for a walk in the woods. |
| 2 | s2 | A girl named Goldilocks found their little house. Knock, knock! Nobody answered, so she walked in. |
| 3 | s3 | She tasted the porridge. "Too hot! Too cold! Just right!" She ate it all up. |
| 4 | s4 | She sat on the chairs. "Too hard! Too soft! Just right!" Crack! The little chair broke. |
| 5 | s5 | She went upstairs and tried the beds. "Too hard! Too soft! Just right!" Soon she was fast asleep. |
| 6 | 🆕A | The bears came home. "Someone's been eating my porridge!" said Papa Bear. "And someone ate mine all up!" cried Baby Bear. |
| 7 | 🆕B | "Someone's been sitting in my chair!" said Mama Bear. "And someone broke mine!" cried Baby Bear. |
| 8 | s6 | They went upstairs. "Someone's sleeping in my bed!" Goldilocks woke up, jumped out of the window, and ran away! |

### 🌳 Level 3 (10)
| # | 그림 | 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s1 | Deep in the forest lived three bears: great big Papa Bear, middle-sized Mama Bear, and wee little Baby Bear. One morning, they made porridge for breakfast. |
| 2 | 🆕C | The porridge was far too hot to eat, so the bears went for a walk while it cooled. |
| 3 | s2 | Meanwhile, a curious girl named Goldilocks came skipping through the woods. She peeked in the window, knocked, and stepped inside. |
| 4 | s3 | She tasted Papa Bear's porridge. "Too hot!" Then Mama Bear's. "Too cold!" Then Baby Bear's. "Just right!" And she gobbled it all up. |
| 5 | s4 | Feeling tired, she tried the chairs. Papa Bear's chair was too hard, and Mama Bear's was too soft. Baby Bear's was just right, until... CRACK! It broke into pieces. |
| 6 | s5 | Upstairs, she tried the beds. The big one was too hard, and the middle one was too soft. The little one was just right, and she fell fast asleep. |
| 7 | 🆕A | Soon the bears came home. "Someone's been eating my porridge!" growled Papa Bear. "Someone's been eating my porridge, too!" said Mama Bear. "And someone ate mine all up!" cried Baby Bear. |
| 8 | 🆕B | "Someone's been sitting in my chair!" said Papa Bear. "Someone's been sitting in mine, too!" said Mama Bear. "And someone broke mine!" sobbed Baby Bear. |
| 9 | s6 | They tiptoed upstairs. "Someone's been sleeping in my bed, and here she is!" squeaked Baby Bear. Goldilocks woke with a scream, leaped out of the window, and ran. |
| 10 | 🆕D | She ran all the way home without looking back, and the three bears never saw her again. |

> 💡 **Level 1도 고칠 후보**: 원작에서 의자는 "too hard, too soft, just right"예요. 지금 Level 1은 "Too big! Too big! Just right!"이라 원작과 달라요. Level 2·3에 맞춰 바꾸는 걸 추천해요. 바꾸면 그 장면 음성만 다시 만들어요.

## 🍪 The Gingerbread Man — 새 그림 4장
원작의 **쌓여 가는 후렴**(*"I've run away from an old woman, an old man, a cow, and a horse…"*)과, 여우가 꼬리 → 등 → 머리 → 코로 올라오게 하는 꾀를 넣었어요. 결말은 원작대로 여우가 먹어요.

| 새 그림 | 장면 |
|---|---|
| 🆕A `farmers` | 밀밭에서 농부들이 낫을 내려놓고 쫓아오고, 진저브레드맨이 뽐내며 달려가는 장면 |
| 🆕B `fox-back` | 강 한가운데서 헤엄치는 여우의 등과 머리 위로 올라가는 진저브레드맨 (물이 점점 차오름) |
| 🆕C `shaping` | 할머니가 반죽을 사람 모양으로 자르고 건포도 눈과 사탕 단추를 붙이는 장면 |
| 🆕D `fox-after` | 강을 건넌 여우가 입맛을 다시며 뿌듯해하고, 물 위에 과자 부스러기 몇 개만 떠 있는 장면 (코믹하게) |

### 🌿 Level 2 (8)
| # | 그림 | 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s1 | An old woman baked a gingerbread man. When she opened the oven, he jumped out and ran away! |
| 2 | s2 | "Run, run, as fast as you can! You can't catch me, I'm the Gingerbread Man!" The old woman and the old man chased him, but he was too fast. |
| 3 | s3 | He ran past a cow and a horse. "Stop! We want to eat you!" But they couldn't catch him. |
| 4 | 🆕A | He ran past some farmers. "I ran away from an old woman, an old man, a cow, and a horse, and I can run away from you, I can!" |
| 5 | s4 | Then he came to a river. "Oh no! I can't swim!" A fox smiled. "Jump on my back. I'll take you across." |
| 6 | 🆕B | The fox began to swim. "You're getting wet. Climb onto my head!" |
| 7 | s5 | "The water is deeper now. Climb onto my nose!" So the Gingerbread Man climbed onto the fox's nose. |
| 8 | s6 | Toss! Snap! The fox gobbled him up. And that was the end of the Gingerbread Man! |

## 🪓 The Golden Axe (금도끼 은도끼) — 새 그림 4장
한국어 그림책처럼 **나무꾼이 도끼를 잃고 우는 장면**, **이웃이 소문을 듣는 장면**을 넣었어요.

| 새 그림 | 장면 |
|---|---|
| 🆕A `cries` | 연못가에 주저앉아 우는 나무꾼 |
| 🆕B `neighbor-hears` | 나무꾼이 마을 사람들에게 금·은도끼를 보여 주고, 욕심쟁이 이웃이 눈이 휘둥그레져 엿듣는 장면 |
| 🆕C `family` | 초가집 앞에서 아내와 아이들에게 인사하고 지게를 지고 산으로 떠나는 가난한 나무꾼 |
| 🆕D `happy-family` | 나무꾼 가족이 초가집 마당에서 웃으며 밥을 먹는 장면, 담 너머로 빈손의 이웃이 시무룩하게 지나감 |

### 🌿 Level 2 (8)
| # | 그림 | 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s1 | Once upon a time, an honest woodcutter was chopping wood by a mountain pond. Splash! His axe fell into the water. |
| 2 | 🆕A | "Oh no! That was my only axe!" The woodcutter sat down and cried. |
| 3 | s2 | Suddenly, a mountain spirit rose from the pond with a golden axe. "Is this your axe?" "No, it isn't." |
| 4 | s3 | The spirit showed him a silver axe. "Is this your axe?" "No, it isn't." Then he showed an old iron axe. "Yes! That's my axe!" |
| 5 | s4 | "You are an honest man. Take all three axes!" The woodcutter went home very happy. |
| 6 | 🆕B | A greedy neighbor heard the story. "I want a golden axe, too!" |
| 7 | s5 | He threw his axe into the pond on purpose. The spirit came up. "Is this your axe?" "Yes! The golden one is mine!" |
| 8 | s6 | "You are lying!" said the spirit, and he disappeared. The greedy neighbor lost his own axe, too. |

| 3 | 🆕A | "What will I do now?" he sighed. "Without my axe, I cannot feed my family." He sat down by the water and wept. |
| 4 | s2 | Suddenly, the pond began to glow, and an old mountain spirit with a long white beard rose from the water. In his hand was a shining golden axe. "Is this the axe you dropped?" "No, sir. That is not mine." |

## 🐦 Heungbu and Nolbu (흥부와 놀부) — 새 그림 4장
한국어 그림책 줄거리대로 **놀부가 흥부를 내쫓는 장면**, **쌀을 얻으러 갔다가 거절당하는 장면**, **제비 새끼가 둥지에서 떨어지는 장면**, **놀부가 뉘우치고 형제가 함께 사는 결말**을 넣었어요.
- 순화: 원작에서 놀부 아내가 주걱으로 흥부 뺨을 때리는 장면은 "문을 쾅 닫는" 것으로, 구렁이가 제비 둥지를 덮치는 장면은 "새끼가 둥지에서 굴러떨어지는" 것으로 부드럽게 했어요. 줄거리는 같아요.

| 새 그림 | 장면 |
|---|---|
| 🆕A `kicked-out` | 기와집 대문 앞에서 놀부가 손가락으로 밖을 가리키고, 흥부 가족이 짐을 지고 떠나는 장면 |
| 🆕B `asks-rice` | 흥부가 놀부 집 문 앞에서 공손히 쌀을 청하고, 놀부가 문을 쾅 닫는 장면 |
| 🆕C `swallow-nest` | 흥부네 초가 처마 밑 제비 둥지, 새끼 제비 한 마리가 둥지에서 떨어지는 순간 |
| 🆕D `brothers` | 진흙투성이 놀부가 무릎 꿇고 사과하고, 흥부가 형을 안아 주는 장면, 뒤에서 흥부 가족이 웃음 |

### 🌿 Level 2 (8)
| # | 그림 | 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s1 | Long ago, there lived two brothers. Nolbu, the older brother, was rich but greedy. Heungbu, the younger brother, was poor but kind. |
| 2 | 🆕A | Nolbu wanted everything for himself. "Get out of my house!" he shouted, and Heungbu's family had to leave. |
| 3 | s2 | One spring day, a baby swallow fell from its nest and hurt its leg. Heungbu gently wrapped it with cloth. "You'll be all right, little one." |
| 4 | s3 | The next spring, the swallow came back with a gourd seed. Heungbu planted it, and big gourds grew on his roof. |
| 5 | s4 | Heungbu and his wife sawed a gourd open. "Saw, saw, let's saw!" Out came rice, gold, and treasure! |
| 6 | s5 | Nolbu heard the news and got jealous. He hurt a swallow's leg on purpose, then wrapped it up. "Now bring me treasure!" |
| 7 | s6 | But when Nolbu sawed his gourds open, out came goblins and mud! "Help! Help!" Nolbu lost everything. |
| 8 | 🆕D | Kind Heungbu took his brother in. "I'm sorry," said Nolbu. From then on, the brothers lived happily together. |

### 🌳 Level 3 (10)
| # | 그림 | 문장 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | s1 | Long ago, in a small village, there lived two brothers. Nolbu, the older one, was rich but very greedy. Heungbu, the younger one, was poor but had a warm heart. |
| 2 | 🆕A | When their parents passed away, Nolbu took the house, the fields, and everything else. "Out you go!" he snapped, and he chased Heungbu's family away. |
| 3 | 🆕B | Heungbu's children were hungry, so he went to his brother. "Please, could you spare a little rice?" But Nolbu slammed the door. "Not one grain!" |
| 4 | 🆕C | One spring, a family of swallows built a nest under Heungbu's roof. One day, a baby swallow tumbled out of the nest and hurt its leg. |
| 5 | s2 | Heungbu picked it up and wrapped its leg with a strip of cloth. "There you go, little one. You'll be all right." Soon, the swallow could fly again. |
| 6 | s3 | When spring came again, the swallow flew back and dropped a gourd seed into Heungbu's hand. He planted it, and enormous gourds grew on his roof. |
| 7 | s4 | Heungbu and his wife sawed the biggest gourd. "Saw, saw, let's saw away!" Out poured rice, gold, silk, and treasure! Their family was never hungry again. |
| 8 | s5 | When Nolbu heard this, he burned with jealousy. He grabbed a swallow, hurt its leg on purpose, and wrapped it up. "Now go and bring me treasure!" |
| 9 | s6 | The swallow brought him a seed, too. But when Nolbu sawed his gourds open, out tumbled mud and naughty goblins, who chased him around and took everything he had. |
