3. The mountain spirit holds a silver axe in one hand and the old iron axe in the other; the woodcutter smiles happily and points to the old iron axe.
4. The woodcutter walks home along a mountain path toward his thatched-roof house at sunset, carrying the golden, silver, and iron axes on his A-frame, beaming.
5. The greedy neighbor tosses his axe into the pond on purpose; the mountain spirit holds up a golden axe and the neighbor eagerly points at it, grinning.
6. The mountain spirit vanishes in a swirl of mist over the pond; the greedy neighbor sits on the bank empty-handed, sulking (not crying hard).
